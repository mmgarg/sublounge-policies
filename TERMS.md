## How SubLounge works

When you send a chat message, SubLounge submits it as a Reddit comment under the chat's Reddit post, using your Reddit account. Your Reddit username and comment are visible according to Reddit's normal comment and community visibility. Text, replies, mentions, and attached images or GIFs may be included in that comment. Review the composer and any first-use disclosure before sending.

SubLounge may show recent comments in a chat interface and keep a limited Redis copy to support chat display, replies, mentions, and unread activity. Reddit remains the place where the submitted comment is published and where Reddit's comment controls and moderation tools apply.
